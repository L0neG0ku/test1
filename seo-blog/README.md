# Kribble SEO package

Everything needed to make kribblecare.com findable when parents in Gurgaon (and later other cities) search for daycares, preschools and playschools.

## Deploy it in three steps

1. Unzip this folder anywhere on your laptop.
2. Open the folder in Claude Code (or a Claude Cowork session with the folder connected).
3. Say: **"Deploy this SEO package to the Kribble website."**

Claude reads `CLAUDE.md`, asks four questions (where the site's code is, how you deploy, the launch date, and whether to publish the first batch), matches the blog to Kribble's design, builds it, applies the homepage fixes with your approval, and deploys when you say yes. You need Python 3.8+, which macOS and Linux already have. Nothing else to install.

## What's inside

- **A 33-post blog (about 33,000 words)** covering how to choose a daycare or preschool, fees, ratios and teaching approaches, safety and verification, working parents (maternity leave, creche at work, backup care, dads and pickup), early-years development, and area guides for Gurgaon (Golf Course Road and DLF, Golf Course Extension and Sohna Road, Sectors 45 to 51, Dwarka Expressway, near-office care), plus three posts for centre owners. 8 posts publish on launch day; 25 follow over three weeks.
- **Technical SEO built in:** titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph and X cards with an image per post, Article/Breadcrumb/FAQ structured data, category pages, RSS, a blog sitemap, and `llms.txt` for AI assistants.
- **Homepage fixes:** a paste-in `<head>` with structured data, and a prioritised checklist. The biggest issue: the site is a single-page app whose served HTML is empty, so Bing, social previews and AI assistants see a blank page. Prerendering fixes it.
- **Keyword map (52 searches) and content calendar.**
- **Outreach kit:** 28 existing parent questions on Quora and forums to answer, weekly Reddit searches, a disclosed reply draft for each, and 20 directories and listings to register on.

## What we need from you

See the open items at the bottom of `FACTS.md`: whether Kribble is free for parents, the Gurgaon launch date, what the verification checklist covers, whether listing is free for centres, social handles, and any field numbers you are happy to publish. The posts mark each one with "TODO".

## Honest expectations

Nobody can guarantee the number one spot. A new domain usually takes two to six months to rank for competitive local searches like "daycare in Gurgaon". Long-tail guides ("questions to ask a daycare", "preschool fees in Gurgaon", "creche facility at work") tend to rank first. "Daycare near me" is won mainly by Google Maps listings and, later, by real centre pages on Kribble, so those come after launch.
